How the Time-Credit Barter Economy Works (Sections 5.2 & 9)

1. Asynchronous Banking

Removes the rigid 'double coincidence of wants'. Teach Docker to User A today, earn a credit, and spend that credit next week learning AWS from User C.

2. Booking Escrow Protection

1 credit is locked when a session is scheduled. It is only released to the mentor when both participants submit completion ratings.

3. Double-Blind Review

Neither party sees the other's review until both have submitted ratings, preventing credit farming and maintaining educational quality.
